# Training illustration assets

The current Android mission uses the versioned portrait backgrounds
`home-practice-v2.png`, `hallway-practice-v2.png` and `street-practice-v2.png`.
See [PORTRAIT_ASSETS.md](PORTRAIT_ASSETS.md) for their final prompts and provenance.
The home view uses one continuous floor and a hallway opening through its rear
wall. Room comparisons and the two-wall explanation are architectural drawings
in Flutter. The older files below are retained as historical assets.

`home-practice.png` and `hallway-practice.png` were generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-03 for the visual redesign. Both are 1536 × 1024. They depict fictional environments and contain no UI, text or safety certification. Native Flutter widgets supply choices, feedback, accessible descriptions and audio actions. The original mission painter remains available for action consequences and asset fallback.

## Home prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: production background illustration for a Flutter child training game, without any UI. Create a polished friendly storybook room in landscape 3:2 composition. Warm cream and peach walls, honey wooden floor, a large window with lavender curtains on the LEFT showing blue sky and greenery, an open exterior wooden door on the RIGHT showing outdoor path and trees, potted plant and small wooden dresser on lower left, small hanging lamp and framed flower. A cheerful thoughtful cartoon girl aged about 8, brown hair, purple shirt with pink heart, blue jeans and purple sneakers stands centered near bottom. Cute rounded shapes and smooth painted shading, crisp high quality children's learning app art, clear readable environment, sunny and calm. Window and door must remain visually distinct on opposite sides; center girl not covering either. No text, no labels, no icons, no checks or crosses, no frame, no device, no buttons, no emergency hazards. This is a fictional scene illustration only. Full bleed art, landscape composition.
